Methodology: Dominion utility donors are identified by matching employer names against known Dominion Energy, Dominion Resources, Dominion Power, and Old Dominion Electric Cooperative variants from VA SBE individual contribution records. Non-utility "Dominion" employers (Old Dominion University, Dominion Medical Associates, Dominion Auto Group, etc.) are excluded. Funding gap = average Dominion $ received by YES voters minus average by NO voters for each bill. Only legislators with recorded votes in va_legislator_recent_votes are included. Dom. Score (0–100) = energy YES-rate weighted by log-scaled Dominion dollar amount — requires both high YES-rate AND large Dominion funding to score high. Correlation does not imply causation. Many energy bills pass with broad bipartisan support, and majority-party members vote YES on most floor bills regardless of donor ties. Use alongside specific bill details, not as a standalone judgment.
