What is the Gatekeeper Effect? When a Finance Committee chair's largest donors are banks and real estate firms — the same industries whose legislation flows through their committee — it raises a structural question: does money follow jurisdiction, or does jurisdiction follow money? An orange-bordered card means the chair's top donor sectors overlap directly with their committee's policy domain. This is a correlation, not a verdict.
