Methodology: Donor industries are classified from the employer field of VA SBE individual contribution records using keyword matching. Bill industries are classified from bill titles in the LegiScan VA 2025–2026 legislative session. Alignment score = % of YES votes on bills classified in the legislator's top donor industry (from va_legislator_recent_votes, final floor votes only). CoI Score (0–100) = alignment weighted by the log-scaled dollar magnitude of the top donor industry — a legislator needs both a high YES-rate and large donations from that industry to score high. A 100% alignment means the legislator voted YES on every bill in that industry — common on bipartisan measures. Higher scores are not inherently problematic — many industry bills pass with broad support, and majority-party members vote YES on most floor bills regardless of donors. Use alongside donor amounts and bill specifics, not as a standalone judgment.
