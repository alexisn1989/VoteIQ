Counter-Signals
High Funding, Low Alignment — Where Money Doesn't Buy Votes
Legislators who receive above-threshold funding from an industry yet vote YES at a rate well below their own party's median for that industry — the clearest cases where the money–vote link breaks down.
⚠ Majority/minority note: Low YES-rates in the minority party partly reflect chamber dynamics, not just donor independence. The within-party-median gap is more meaningful than the raw alignment number alone.
No legislators currently meet the threshold (>$8K funded, <50% alignment).
Methodology: Donor industries classified from VA SBE Schedule A employer fields via keyword matching (2024–2025 cycles). Bill industries from LegiScan VA 2025–2026 session bill titles. CoI Score (0–100) = (YES-rate in top donor industry) × log-scaled dollar magnitude. Sponsor pass rate = Passed bills ÷ total classified primary-sponsored bills. Dominion filter = employer names matching Dominion Energy utility patterns, excluding non-utility Dominion-named businesses. All correlations are observational — this analysis does not infer causation, improper influence, or intent. Donor–Legislation detail · Dominion full analysis
